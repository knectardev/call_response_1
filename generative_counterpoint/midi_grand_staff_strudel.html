About MIDI transcription: MIDI records pitches, timing, velocity, tracks, and optional tempo / meter / key metadata, but usually not note spelling, voices, fingering, or clef assignment. This viewer quantizes notation to the selected grid; playback retains the file’s original timing and velocities. Tuplets, overlapping voices and highly expressive playing may require manual engraving for a publication-quality score.
